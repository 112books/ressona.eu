**Own cookies**

This website does not use its own tracking or advertising cookies.

**Analytics**

We use **GoatCounter** for audience analytics. GoatCounter does not collect cookies or personal data. More information at [goatcounter.com](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

**Third-party cookies — Google reCAPTCHA**

The homepage includes **Google reCAPTCHA v3** to protect the subscription form against automated spam submissions. reCAPTCHA is a service by Google LLC that analyses user behaviour in the browser and sets Google's own cookies (such as `_GRECAPTCHA`) to determine whether the user is a person or a bot. These cookies serve a strictly technical and security purpose.

The use of reCAPTCHA involves transmitting data to Google's servers in the USA. You can consult Google's privacy policy at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Third-party cookies — Sibforms/Brevo**

The homepage includes the newsletter subscription form from **Brevo (Sibforms)**. This service may set functional cookies for the correct operation of the form. Brevo's privacy policy: [brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy](https://www.brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/).

**Managing cookies**

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Note that blocking Google cookies may prevent the subscription form from working. The rest of the website is fully functional without cookies.

**More information**

For any questions about our use of cookies, write to us at hola@ressona.eu.
