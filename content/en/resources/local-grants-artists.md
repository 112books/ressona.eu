EU and INAEM grants attract most attention because the amounts are large. But local calls — from provincial councils, county councils and city halls — tend to have fewer applications, more accessible criteria and a more direct connection to the territory where you work. For many artists, especially at early stages, this is the fastest route to real public funding.

## Diputació de Barcelona

The **[Diputació de Barcelona](https://www.diba.cat/web/cultura)** manages two types of grants relevant to artists:

**Artistic creation and cultural production grants**: for creation projects in any discipline with impact on municipalities in the Barcelona demarcation. Particularly values territorial distribution and decentralisation.

**Xarxa de Teatres**: the Diputació manages the network of municipal theatres and auditoriums in the demarcation. Companies that enter the network access a guaranteed programming schedule in municipalities across the province. Access is by open call. Highly relevant for theatre, dance and live music companies.

## Diputació de Girona, Tarragona and Lleida

All four Catalan provincial councils publish annual cultural calls. Deadlines and amounts vary, but the pattern is similar: grants for production, distribution and programming within the demarcation.

- **[Diputació de Girona](https://www.ddgi.cat/cultura/)**: particular attention to popular culture, traditional music and contemporary performing arts.
- **[Diputació de Tarragona](https://www.dipta.cat/)**: territory-linked programmes, proximity festivals and cultural heritage.
- **[Diputació de Lleida](https://www.diputaciolleida.cat/)**: rural culture, traditions and Pyrenean traditional music.

## City councils: Barcelona, Terrassa, Sabadell, Girona, Tarragona

**Barcelona**: beyond ICUB, Barcelona City Council manages **General Grants Calls** that open each year in the first quarter. There are specific lines for cultural projects in each district. A culturally interesting project without a candidacy for the general lines may have access to district calls with less competition.

**Terrassa**, **Sabadell**, **Girona**, **Tarragona**: each has annual grants calls for cultural entities and projects. Search the electronic office of each city council for "subvencions cultura."

## How to find local calls

No centralised resource collects all local cultural calls in Catalonia. The search is manual:

1. Google: `[city council or provincial council name] + subvencions cultura + [current year]`
2. Each body's electronic office: usually has a "notice board" or "grants" section where call terms are published.
3. The **[DOGC — Official Gazette of Catalonia](https://portaldogc.gencat.cat/)**: all public grant calls in Catalonia are published in the DOGC. Setting up keyword alerts ("subvenció" + "cultura" + "arts") prevents missing deadlines.

## The unwritten rule

Local calls reward proximity and territorial commitment. A project demonstrating knowledge of the local context, connections with local organisations and clear community benefit scores far above a project of artistic excellence but without local roots. This is not the same logic as INAEM or Creative Europe.
