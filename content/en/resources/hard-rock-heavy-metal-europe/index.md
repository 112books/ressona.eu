There are two ways to enter European hard rock. The **fan** who wants to know where heavy metal was born, which festival is worth the trip, which club in Birmingham or Oslo won't appear in any tourist guide. And the **musician** who wants to understand the circuit: which festivals programme emerging bands, where to send proposals, what the real path looks like for a metal band in Catalonia or Spain.

This guide covers both perspectives. From the cradle of hard rock — Birmingham, 1968 — to the radical Basque punk scene, the Catalan-language heavy metal of Sangtraït, and the world-reference festivals. With space for neighbourhood clubs, for the venues that made rock possible in Barcelona, and for the circuits a hard rock musician can travel today.

## Scenes by territory

<details>
<summary><strong>Barcelona and Catalonia</strong> — cradle of hard rock in Catalan</summary>

Barcelona has one of Europe's most singular hard rock scenes: it is the only place in the world where heavy metal took root as a vehicle for expression in a minority language. From Sala Zeleste to Razzmatazz, from La Banda Trapera del Río to Sangtraït, the timeline of rock in the Catalan capital is inseparable from its political and cultural history.

### Timeline

| Period | Key moment |
|---|---|
| 1975 | **Sala Zeleste** opens in Poblenou. First platform for hard rock in Barcelona. |
| 1976 | **La Banda Trapera del Río** (Cornellà) — first punk band in Spain. First hard rock song in Catalan: *Ciutat Podrida*. |
| 1982 | **Sangtraït** forms in La Jonquera. Heavy metal in Catalan. *L'Últim Segell* (1991): 90,000 copies and 22,104 people at Palau Sant Jordi. |
| 1986 | **Sopa de Cabra** forms (Girona/Barcelona). Hard rock in Catalan, defining act of the 1990s. |
| 1980s | Hardcore scene: **L'Odi Social**, **Subterranean Kids**, **Anti/Dogmatikss**, **GRB** — pioneers of Catalan hardcore punk. |
| 1990s | **24 Ideas** introduces New York Hardcore (NYHC) and straight edge to Catalonia. |
| 2000 | **Razzmatazz** inherits Zeleste's spirit in the same neighbourhood. Five rooms, 2,100 capacity. |
| 2014 | **Rock Fest Barcelona** launches at Can Zam (Santa Coloma de Gramenet). Up to 50,000 attendees. Iron Maiden, Judas Priest, Scorpions, Kiss. |

### Active venues

| Venue | Location | Capacity | Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Razzmatazz](https://www.salarazzmatazz.com/) | Poblenou | ~2,100 (5 rooms) | Hard rock, metal, punk, indie. Spiritual successor to Zeleste |
| [Sala Apolo](https://www.sala-apolo.com/) | Poble-sec | ~1,500 | 80 years of history. Hard rock since the 70s, eclectic programming |
| [Sala Bikini](https://www.bikini.cat/) | Les Corts | ~1,000 | International hard rock and metal |
| [Sala Upload](https://www.sala-upload.com/) | Poble-sec | ~800 | Hard rock, metal, emerging hardcore |

### Festivals

| Festival | Location | Month | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Rock Fest Barcelona](https://rockfestbarcelona.com/) | Can Zam, Santa Coloma | June/July | International reference festival. Global headliners |
| [Canet Rock](https://www.canetrock.cat/) | Canet de Mar | July | Catalan rock reference since 1975 |
| [Resurrection Fest](https://www.resurrectionfest.es/) | Viveiro, Galicia | July | Extreme metal, 30,000+ attendees |

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Spain</strong> — regional scenes and pioneering bands</summary>

Spain's hard rock scene was forged during the democratic transition. Madrid produced classic heavy metal (Barón Rojo, Obús); the Basque Country generated radical punk; Galicia gave the humour and social critique of Siniestro Total; Extremadura produced the transgressive expressiveness of Extremoduro.

### Madrid

**Barón Rojo** — heavy metal since 1980, pioneers in Spain. **Obús** — heavy metal, same generation. **Mägo de Oz** — folk metal, one of the best-selling Spanish metal bands in history. **Saratoga** and **WarCry** — reference power metal.

Clubs and venues: [Sala Caracol](https://www.salacaracol.com/), Sala But (historic). Festivals: **Download Madrid** (2017–2019, Spanish edition of the British Download).

### Basque Country

The radical Basque rock scene is a unique chapter in European music: punk and hardcore tied to the political struggle of a people under repression. **Eskorbuto** (Santurtzi, 1980) — an absolute legend. **RIP**, **Kortatu**, **Soziedad Alkoholika** and **BAP!!** (Brigada Anti Polizialak) — the founding body of radical rock.

Reference venues: **Sala Jimmy Jazz** (Vitoria) and **Kafe Antzokia** (Bilbao).

### Galicia

**Siniestro Total** (Vigo, 1981) — punk rock with humour and social critique, one of the most beloved bands in Spain. A recognised influence on the entire following generation.

### Asturias / Extremadura

**Extremoduro** (Plasencia, 1987) — transgressive hard rock, lyrics of unusual literary density for rock. An absolute reference for three decades until the band dissolved in 2014.

### Valencian Country

**Tierra Santa** — reference European power metal, born in Valencia in the 1990s.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>United Kingdom</strong> — the cradle</summary>

All hard rock comes from here. **Birmingham** gave birth to Black Sabbath (1968) and heavy metal as a genre. **London** gave punk (Sex Pistols, The Clash, The Damned) and the NWOBHM (Iron Maiden, Saxon). **Liverpool** and **Manchester** fed hard rock with the blues.

**Founding bands:** Led Zeppelin, Deep Purple, Black Sabbath, The Who, Motörhead, Iron Maiden, Judas Priest, Sex Pistols, The Clash.

**Genres originating here:** hard rock, heavy metal, punk rock, NWOBHM, doom metal, d-beat, grindcore.

**Festivals:**

| Festival | Location | Month | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Download Festival](https://downloadfestival.co.uk/) | Donington Park | June | The world's reference hard rock and metal festival. Successor to Monsters of Rock (1980–1996). 110,000+ attendees |
| [Bloodstock](https://www.bloodstock.uk.com/) | Walton-on-Trent | August | Pure heavy metal, 20,000 attendees, strong commitment to underground scenes |

**Historic venues:** Marquee Club (London), Rainbow Theatre (London), Hammersmith Odeon.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Germany</strong> — power metal, thrash and Wacken</summary>

Germany is the country that has exported the most metal in Europe outside the UK: Teutonic thrash (Kreator, Sodom, Destruction), power metal (Helloween, Blind Guardian), global hard rock (Scorpions) and industrial metal (Rammstein).

**Bands:** Scorpions, Accept, Rammstein, Kreator, Sodom, Destruction, Helloween, Blind Guardian.

**Festivals:**

| Festival | Location | Month | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wacken Open Air](https://www.wacken.com/) | Wacken | August | The world's largest metal festival. Since 1990, 80,000+ attendees. Every variant of metal |
| [Rock am Ring / Rock im Park](https://www.rock-am-ring.com/) | Nürburgring / Nuremberg | June | Double festival, 80,000+ total attendees |

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>France</strong> — Hellfest and extreme metal</summary>

France has an extreme metal scene of enormous quality: **Gojira** (progressive death metal, the Radiohead of metal), **Alcest** (black metal/shoegaze, truly unique), **Deathspell Omega** and **Blut Aus Nord** (avant-garde black metal). And it has **Hellfest**, the world's reference extreme metal festival.

| Festival | Location | Month | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Hellfest](https://www.hellfest.fr/) | Clisson, Loire-Atlantique | June | The world's reference extreme metal festival. Since 2006. 60,000+ attendees. Death, black, thrash, doom. Everything in a permanent site |

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Scandinavia</strong> — black metal, melodic death metal and power metal</summary>

### Norway — black metal

Norway produced the Second Wave of Black Metal (1991–1995), the most controversial and influential episode in European metal: **Mayhem**, **Burzum**, **Darkthrone**, **Emperor**, **Satyricon**, **Gorgoroth**. Church burnings, ideological controversy and an aesthetic that permeated metal for decades to come.

### Sweden — the Gothenburg sound

Melodic death metal was born in Gothenburg in the 1990s: **In Flames**, **At the Gates**, **Dark Tranquillity**. Alongside: **Meshuggah** (djent), **Opeth** (progressive metal), **Entombed** and **Dismember** (Floridian death metal adopted and transformed).

### Finland — symphonic metal and gothic metal

**Nightwish** — symphonic metal with operatic vocals. **HIM** — love metal, the style that spread across Europe. **Children of Bodom**, **Amorphis**, **Stratovarius**.

### Denmark

**Mercyful Fate** and **King Diamond** (occultist heavy metal, influence on all of black metal). [Roskilde](https://www.roskilde-festival.dk/) (1971, 130,000+ attendees) is Scandinavia's largest multi-genre festival, with a strong hard rock component. [Copenhell](https://www.copenhell.dk/) (Copenhagen) is the reference Scandinavian metal event.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Rest of Europe</strong> — Belgium, Switzerland, Poland, Portugal...</summary>

**Belgium:** [Graspop Metal Meeting](https://www.graspop.be/) (Dessel, June) — one of Europe's reference extreme metal festivals. 50,000+ attendees. Channel Zero, dEUS.

**Switzerland:** Celtic Frost (foundational extreme metal), Krokus (hard rock). **[Paléo Festival](https://www.paleo.ch/)** (Nyon, July) — not purely metal, but includes hard rock sections.

**Poland:** A first-rate extreme metal scene — **Behemoth** (blackened death metal), **Vader** (death metal), **Decapitated** (technical death metal). [Pol'and'Rock](https://polandrock.pl/) (formerly Woodstock Poland, August) — up to 500,000 attendees, one of Europe's largest festivals.

**Portugal:** **Moonspell** (gothic metal, Iberian reference). [Sudowest TMN / NOS Alive](https://www.nosalive.com/) includes hard rock sections.

**Romania:** **Negură Bunget** (atmospheric black metal, unique in its roots in Romanian tradition).

</details>

## For musicians: how the European hard rock circuit works

The hard rock circuit is one of the most stratified in live music. From a 60-person pub to Wacken's 80,000, there are clearly defined tiers and each one has its own rules.

**The starting point is the local scene.** Barcelona has Razzmatazz, Sala Upload, Sala Bikini. Madrid has Sala Caracol. The Basque Country has Kafe Antzokia. No Spanish band has entered the European circuit without first passing through the venues of their own territory.

**Emerging metal festivals open the circuit.** The British **Bloodstock** and the Galician **Resurrection Fest** (Viveiro) have open calls for emerging bands. **Rock Fest Barcelona** accepts proposals for its support stages. This is where many bands make the leap.

**Discography matters.** In hard rock, having recordings (physical album, EP, active Bandcamp page) is part of credibility. Festival promoters consult [Encyclopaedia Metallum](https://www.metal-archives.com/) — the global metal archive — as a first check on track record. Having a profile there is obligatory.

**The rider is your work contract.** In hard rock, the technical rider (PA, backline, lights) is especially important: concerts with significant production require prior coordination. Not having a written rider means not being professional.
