Flamenco is not just a musical style: it is a way of feeling. Lorca called it "duende" — that deep emotion born from the earth and erupting in the body of the cantaor, the guitarist, the bailaor. Chasing the duende means travelling to the places where flamenco is still alive, where the walls have absorbed centuries of soleá, bulería and seguiriya.

Here is a guide to discovering the best of flamenco, city by city. The starting point is **Barcelona**: four tablaos, four peñas and its own festivals — Flamenco de Barrio in Nou Barris, Ciutat Flamenco at the Taller de Músics, the Festival de Nou Barris — making the city a far more vibrant flamenco hub than it might seem. From here, the route extends to the most authentic peñas of Jerez, the Andalusian palaces of Seville and the festivals that transform streets and squares into stages of unbridled passion.

## Tablaos and peñas by city

<details>
<summary><strong>Barcelona</strong> — 4 tablaos · 4 peñas</summary>

Barcelona is not native flamenco territory, but it has embraced the art form and made it its own. Since the 1960s, the city has been a meeting point for flamenco artists who found here an open and demanding audience.

**[Tablao Flamenco Cordobés](https://www.tablaocordobes.com/)**
La Rambla 35, 08002 Barcelona

Founded in 1970. Interior decorated with azulejo tiles. Has hosted Farruco, Chocolate, Manzanita and Lole y Manuel. Several sessions every night.

**[Tablao de Carmen](https://www.tablaodecarmen.com/)**
Avinguda Paral·lel 62, 08001 Barcelona (Poble Espanyol)

A tribute to Carmen Amaya. Founded in 1988. Traditional and contemporary flamenco in an Andalusian courtyard with restaurant.

**[Palacio del Flamenco](https://www.palaciodelflamenco.com/)**
Carrer de Balmes 139, 08008 Barcelona

Large-format venue, over 300 seats. Ideal for groups and events. Spectacular shows.

**[Los Tarantos](https://www.tablaolostarantos.com/)**
Plaça Reial 17, 08002 Barcelona

Over 50 years of history at the heart of the Gothic Quarter. Intimate and authentic.

**[Peña Flamenca Catalana](https://www.penaflamencacatalana.com/)** · Carrer de la Riereta 7, 08001
Founded in 1964. One of the oldest peñas in Catalonia. Concerts, competitions, classes.

**Peña Flamenca La Planta** · Barcelona — Regular programme of concerts and activities.

**Peña Flamenca Fosforito** · Barcelona — A tribute to the great cantaor Fosforito.

**Peña Flamenca Niño de la Huerta** · Barcelona — Dedicated to the memory of the cantaor.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Madrid</strong> — 9 tablaos · 1 peña</summary>

Madrid is the undisputed capital of flamenco. Since the late nineteenth century, the city's tablaos have hosted the great names of the genre: Carmen Amaya, Antonio el Bailarín, Paco de Lucía, Camarón de la Isla.

**[Corral de la Morería](https://www.corraldelamoreria.com/)**
Calle de la Morería 17, 28005 Madrid

Founded in 1956, it is the oldest and most prestigious flamenco tablao in the world. Its stage has seen Isabel Pantoja, Lola Flores, Antonio Gades and Blanca del Rey. Two sessions every night. Restaurant directed by David García (Michelin star).

**[Casa Patas](https://www.casapatas.com/)**
Calle de los Cañizares 10, 28012 Madrid

Since 1985. A temple of pure flamenco: no microphones, no amplification. Has hosted Farruquito, Estrella Morente, Diego el Cigala and José Mercé.

**[Villa Rosa](https://www.villarosa.es/)**
Plaza de Santa Ana 15, 28012 Madrid

Founded in 1911. Walls covered in azulejo tiles depicting flamenco scenes. One of Madrid's most iconic venues.

**[Torres Bermejas](https://www.torresbermejas.com/)**
Calle de Mesonero Romanos 11, 28013 Madrid

Interior that recreates the Alhambra of Granada. Has hosted Manolo Caracol, Camarón and Lola Flores.

**[Café de Chinitas](https://www.cafedechinitas.com/)**
Calle de Torija 7, 28013 Madrid

A tribute to the famous copla by Federico García Lorca. Intimate, family-friendly atmosphere.

**[Cardamomo](https://www.cardamomo.es/)**
Calle de Echegaray 15, 28014 Madrid

Contemporary flamenco that respects its roots. Three sessions every night, masterclasses and workshops.

**[Tablao Flamenco 1911](https://www.tablao1911.com/)**
Calle de la Paz 10, 28012 Madrid

Modernist building from 1911. Capacity for 80 people. Intimate and authentic space.

**[Las Carboneras](https://www.lascarboneras.com/)**
Plaza del Conde de Miranda 1, 28005 Madrid

A sixteenth-century palace in the La Latina neighbourhood. Stone vaults, unique acoustics.

**[Tablao Flamenco La Quimera](https://www.laquimeraflamenco.com/)**
Calle de la Salud 20, 28013 Madrid

Solid programming, affordable prices, established artists and rising talent.

**Peña La Platería** · Madrid
A cultural association dedicated to spreading flamenco. Concerts, workshops and activities.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Seville</strong> — 5 tablaos · 3 peñas</summary>

Seville is the birthplace of flamenco. This is where siguiriya was born, where the first peñas flamencas were founded, where people still dance for alegría in the streets of Triana and Macarena.

**[El Palacio Andaluz](https://www.elpalacioandaluz.com/)**
Plaza de la Encarnación, Sevilla

Over 20 artists on stage. Grand production of cante, toque, baile and live music. Ideal for those seeking a complete flamenco experience.

**[Tablao El Arenal](https://www.tablaoelarenal.es/)**
Calle Rodo 7, 41001 Sevilla

Founded in 1975 in the El Arenal neighbourhood. Has hosted Cristina Hoyos, Antonio Canales and Javier Barón. Intimate atmosphere, authentic experience.

**[Los Gallos](https://www.tablaolosgallos.com/)**
Plaza de Santa Cruz 11, 41004 Sevilla

Founded in 1966. One of the few tablaos with daily programming all year round.

**[La Casa del Flamenco](https://www.lacasadelflamencosevilla.com/)**
Calle Ximénez de Enciso 28, 41004 Sevilla

Fifteenth-century palace in the Santa Cruz neighbourhood. Andalusian courtyard, total proximity to the artists.

**[El Patio Sevillano](https://www.elpatiossevillano.es/)**
Paseo de Cristóbal Colón 11, 41001 Sevilla

Views of the Guadalquivir and the Torre del Oro. Traditional flamenco and contemporary proposals.

**Peña Flamenca La Bulería** · Sevilla — Concerts, cante competitions. Family atmosphere, far from the tourist circuit.

**Peña Torres Macarena** · Sevilla — Historic peña from the Macarena neighbourhood. Regular programming.

**Peña El Cerro** · Sevilla — In the Cerro neighbourhood. Concerts, classes and gatherings for the local community.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Granada</strong> — 5 tablaos · 2 peñas</summary>

Granada is flamenco from the caves of Sacromonte to the taverns of the Albaicín. Here flamenco was forged among Moors, Romani and Jewish communities.

**[Jardines de Zoraya](https://www.jardinesdezoraya.com/)**
Calle Panaderos 32, 18010 Granada

In the Albaicín, with views of the Alhambra. Andalusian courtyard, fountain, exceptional natural acoustics. Restaurant serving traditional Granadan cuisine.

**[Tablao Flamenco Albayzín](https://www.tablaoalbayzin.com/)**
Calle Panaderos 14, 18010 Granada

At the heart of the Albaicín. Traditional flamenco in a historic setting.

**[La Soleá](https://www.tablaolasolea.com/)**
Calle del Cardenal 5, 18009 Granada

One of Granada's most traditional tablaos. Pure flamenco, intimate atmosphere.

**[Cuevas Los Tarantos](https://www.cuevaslostarantos.es/)**
Camino del Sacromonte 9, 18010 Granada

Shows performed in caves carved into the hillside. The sound of the guitar resonates off the stone walls. One of the most authentic flamenco experiences in existence.

**[Ventas El Gallo](https://www.ventaselgallo.com/)**
Calle Panaderos 40, 18010 Granada

Andalusian courtyard with views of the Alhambra. Flamenco and traditional Andalusian cuisine.

**[Peña Flamenca La Platería](https://www.laplateria.org.es/)** · Placeta de Toqueros 7, 18010
Founded in 1949. The oldest peña in Granada. Has hosted Enrique Morente and Estrella Morente. Weekly concerts.

**Peña Flamenca El Chato** · Granada — Regular programming in the city centre.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Jerez de la Frontera</strong> — 5 peñas</summary>

Jerez is the mecca of flamenco. This is where Camarón de la Isla, Lola Flores, Manuel Torre and La Paquera de Jerez were born. The peñas of Jerez attract no tourists — only aficionados who live flamenco as a religion.

**Peña Flamenca La Bulería** · Calle de la Bulería, Jerez — Regular concerts, purely flamenco atmosphere.

**Peña Flamenca Tío José de Paula** · Jerez — A tribute to the great cantaor. Traditional Jerez-style flamenco.

**Peña Flamenca El Garbanzo de Jerez** · Jerez — Regular programming for the local community.

**Peña Flamenca Niño de la Huerta** · Jerez — Concerts and gatherings in memory of the cantaor.

**Peña Flamenca El Zoco** · Jerez — A gathering place for aficionados.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Málaga</strong> — 2 tablaos</summary>

**[Kelipe Centro de Arte Flamenco](https://www.kelipe.net/)**
Calle Calderería 6, 29008 Málaga

Founded by José Luis Pérez-Vera, guitarist and researcher. Intimate space in the historic centre. Shows, guitar, cante and baile classes. Committed to local and emerging artists.

**Tablao Flamenco Los Amayas** · Málaga — Traditional flamenco in an authentic atmosphere.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Córdoba</strong> — 2 tablaos</summary>

**[Tablao El Cardenal](https://www.tablaoelcardenal.com/)**
Calle de Torrijos 10, 14003 Córdoba

Next to the Mosque-Cathedral. Typical Andalusian courtyard, solid artistic quality.

**Tablao Flamenco El Tablao** · Córdoba — Traditional flamenco in the historic centre.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>London</strong> — 1 tablao · 1 peña</summary>

London has a surprisingly vibrant flamenco scene, a product of Spanish immigration and British curiosity.

**[Peña Flamenca de Londres](https://www.penaflamencadelondres.com/)**
London, United Kingdom

The oldest flamenco peña in the United Kingdom. Concerts, guitar and baile classes. Spanish artists passing through and local talent.

**[Ronnie Scott's Jazz Club](https://www.ronniescotts.co.uk/)**
47 Frith Street, London W1D 4HT

Jazz club that occasionally programmes flamenco and flamenco fusion during festivals and special events.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Paris</strong> — 2 tablaos</summary>

Paris has always been a meeting point for flamenco artists.

**[Tablao Flamenco de París](https://www.tablaoflamencodeparis.fr/)**
Paris, France — Traditional flamenco for French and international audiences.

**Casa Patas (París)** · Paris — An offshoot of the legendary Casa Patas in Madrid. Authentic flamenco in an intimate setting.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Amsterdam</strong> — 1 peña</summary>

**Pena Flamenca de Amsterdam** · Amsterdam, Netherlands
Active peña with a regular programme of concerts, classes and flamenco activities.

</details>
