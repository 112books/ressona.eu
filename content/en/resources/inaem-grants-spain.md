The **[INAEM — National Institute of Performing Arts and Music](https://www.culturaydeporte.gob.es/inaem/portada.html)** is the body within Spain's Ministry of Culture that manages the bulk of state funding for the performing arts and music. The grants it awards annually represent tens of millions of euros in direct support for artists, companies, venues and festivals. Many artists outside Spain — including those based in Catalonia — never apply. Some don't know the programmes exist; others assume, not entirely wrongly, that the system favours large structures. But there are calls designed for individual artists and small companies that are worth knowing.

## Main INAEM programmes

**Grants for creation and production in the performing arts**: subsidies for producing new works in theatre, dance, circus and street arts. Includes creation residencies, support for dramaturgy and backing for experimental formats.

**Distribution and touring grants**: for companies wanting to tour. Includes access to the Red de Teatros de España (Spain's national theatre network) and support for presence at professional markets like FIRA de Tàrrega, BiTes and the main state festivals.

**Music grants**: the broadest programme, with lines for music production, recording, distribution and festival presence. Includes specific support for jazz, contemporary classical music, traditional music and fusion.

**Residency and mobility grants**: INAEM collaborates with national and international residency centres to offer grants for artists in creation phases.

**Circus grants**: specific programmes for contemporary circus and street arts companies.

**Visual arts grants**: grants for curators, visual artists and exhibition projects.

## How to apply

Calls open once or twice a year, usually in the first quarter (January–February) for projects in the current year. Applications are submitted entirely through the **[Ministry of Culture Electronic Office](https://sede.mcu.gob.es/)**, using a digital certificate or Cl@ve.

Standard requirements:
- **Registered as self-employed** or constituted as a legal entity (association, SL, SA, cooperative). Individual applications without social security registration rarely succeed.
- **Spanish NIF or CIF** in force.
- **Up to date with tax and social security obligations**.
- **Project documentation**: artistic statement, itemised budget, CV of the artist or company, letter of intent or distribution commitment.

## Ministry of Culture: other programmes

Beyond INAEM, the Ministry of Culture manages:

- **Music publishing grants** (for independent labels and self-publishing artists).
- **Spanish Academy of Fine Arts in Rome fellowships**: for visual artists, architects and musicians. Highly competitive, highly valued.
- **Plan for the Promotion of the Music Industry**: support for internationalisation of Spanish music through ICEX and other channels.

## Common application mistakes

**Budget without detailed justification.** INAEM rejects or rates poorly global budget lines ("production: €15,000") without itemised breakdown.

**Generic artistic statement.** A good statement explains the specific project to be funded, not the company's general history. The evaluator wants to know exactly where the money will go and what it will produce.

**Applying for already-incurred costs.** Public grants fund future or ongoing projects, not already-paid costs.
