The performing arts — dance, theatre, circus and street arts — have their own funding ecosystem, partially overlapping with general cultural programmes but with sector-specific calls worth knowing separately. The difference between finding funding and not finding it often comes down to knowing where to look.

## Dance

**INAEM — Dance Programme**: the Ministry of Culture funds production of new creation, touring within Spain and participation in international festivals. Dance-specific calls include grants for co-production, residency and advanced training.

**[Centro Coreográfico Nacional (CND)](https://www.cndanza.mcu.es/)**: not a grant programme in the strict sense, but the CND offers creation residencies for Spanish companies. Access is by public call.

**[Aerowaves](https://aerowaves.org/)**: European distribution network for contemporary dance. Being selected for the Aerowaves Spring Forward Festival is a direct door to programmers from 34 European countries. Not a financial grant but access to the circuit — which is worth as much or more.

**[CoNCA](https://conca.gencat.cat/)**: residency and mobility grants explicitly include dance companies.

## Theatre

**INAEM — Theatre and Circus Programme**: grants for production, distribution, residency and training in theatre and circus.

**[Teatro Nacional de Cataluña (TNC)](https://www.tnc.cat/)** and **[Centro Dramático Nacional (CDN)](https://cdn.mcu.es/)**: co-production and creation residencies for theatre companies with a track record. Not accessible for first companies without history.

**[ASSITEJ España](https://www.assitej.es/)**: association for theatre for children and young people, with training, distribution and international TYA circuit connection programmes.

**[ITI — International Theatre Institute](https://www.iti-worldwide.org/)**: for companies wanting connection to the UNESCO international theatre circuit.

## Circus

**INAEM — Circus and Street Arts Programme**: specific funding for production and distribution of contemporary circus. Includes presence at professional markets like FIRA de Tàrrega.

**[Circostrada](https://www.circostrada.org/)**: the European network for contemporary circus and street arts. Manages residency, mobility and funding programmes through its member entities. **FIRA de Tàrrega** is a Circostrada member, meaning Catalan companies have privileged access to the network.

**[CircusNext](https://www.circusnext.eu/)**: European programme supporting contemporary circus creation, managed by Jeune Cirque Europe. For European companies on their first productions.

## Cross-disciplinary performing arts programmes

**[IETM — International Network for Contemporary Performing Arts](https://www.ietm.org/)**: international network of contemporary performing arts professionals. Membership gives access to calls and connections that rarely arrive through other channels.

## Professional markets: indirect funding

Being selected for a professional market is not a grant, but it is indirect funding:

- **[FIRA de Tàrrega](https://www.firatarrega.cat/)**: the main circus and street arts market in Spain. Each selection generates real contracting opportunities.
- **[BiTes](https://bites.cat/)**: professional market for Catalan performing arts.
- **[WOMEX](https://www.womex.com/)**: world music market with performing arts components.
