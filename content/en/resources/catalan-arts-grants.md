Catalonia has three main bodies that manage public cultural funding: CoNCA for grants to individual artists and creative projects, ICEC for support to companies and projects with a professional dimension, and ICUB for projects tied to the city of Barcelona. Each has its own objectives, criteria and deadlines — understanding the difference is the first step to knowing where to apply.

## CoNCA — Consell Nacional de la Cultura i les Arts

**[CoNCA](https://conca.gencat.cat/)** is the independent Catalan body that manages direct grants to individual artists and creators. Its main calls:

**Research and creation grants**: for artists wanting to dedicate a period to a specific creative project. Cover production costs, research and materials. Usual duration: one year. Open to all artistic disciplines.

**Artist residency grants**: partnerships with national and international residency spaces to facilitate access for Catalan artists to concentrated work periods outside their usual environment.

**International mobility grants**: for artists wanting to participate in festivals, professional markets, residencies or training abroad. Cover travel and accommodation costs.

CoNCA publishes its calls on the **[Tràmits Gencat](https://web.gencat.cat/ca/tramits/)** portal and its own website. Applications are submitted electronically.

## ICEC — Institut Català de les Empreses Culturals

**[ICEC](https://icec.gencat.cat/)** is different from CoNCA: while CoNCA funds artists and creators, ICEC funds cultural companies and projects with an economic dimension. Its focus is professionalisation of the sector.

Key programmes:

**Performing arts production grants**: for companies and producers producing new shows with a professional distribution vocation. Particularly values projects with confirmed touring commitments or co-production agreements.

**Distribution and export grants**: for companies wanting to tour Spain and internationalise. Covers logistics, translation, subtitling and presence at international fairs.

**Music and recording production grants**: for recording, distribution and promotion projects by professional Catalan musicians.

Unlike CoNCA, accessing ICEC grants requires a professional business structure — at minimum, being self-employed and registered in the corresponding cultural activity.

## ICUB — Institut de Cultura de Barcelona

**[ICUB](https://www.barcelona.cat/barcelonacultura/ca)** is the municipal equivalent of ICEC for the city of Barcelona. Its calls target projects with a Barcelona connection:

**General Grants Call**: the main mechanism of Barcelona City Council for cultural, sports and community projects. Cultural lines cover artistic production, performing arts, music, visual arts and heritage.

**Fàbriques de Creació**: municipal creation and residency spaces (Fabra i Coats, Hangar, La Caldera, Antic Teatre, L'Estruch). Access by public call. They offer space, time and in some cases economic support.

## Diputació de Barcelona and Consells Comarcals

For territory-based projects, the **[Diputació de Barcelona](https://www.diba.cat/)** and Consells Comarcals (county councils) manage grants that many artists overlook. Especially relevant for traditional music projects, cultural activities in small municipalities and proximity-based projects.
