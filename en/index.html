— 03

Who we are

Ressona is a project by Joan Mz, built in Barcelona from three complementary areas of expertise. A broad perspective for building digital identity for artists.

Gaze

The first layer of any artist is visual. At Pocallum they work on artistic photography, logo design and visual identity that communicate before words do.

Structure

Talent needs a solid foundation. At LinuxBCN they design the digital infrastructure: websites, automation, applied AI and open source software that make your project visible.

Narrative

At 112books they bring editorial judgment and experience in cultural communication. Every artist is a unique publication deserving a coherent, lasting story.
